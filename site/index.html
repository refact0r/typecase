typecase is a collection of the typefaces i like from around the internet, and a place to compare them side by side.

everything here aims to be open source or free to use, loaded from google fonts, fontshare, or hosted here under open licenses. fonts marked * are proprietary: they aren't hosted, and only show up if you have them installed.

seen a font you like somewhere else? paste the url of its font file (find it in the network tab of your browser's dev tools) into the field at the top left to try it out here. it's only loaded in your browser, for as long as the page is open.
